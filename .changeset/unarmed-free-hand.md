**Unarmed Combat** — A grab, a punch and a limb block are offered only while a hand is free to perform them, and one free hand is enough.

**Unarmed Combat** — A creature grabs, punches or raises a limb to block only where it has a limb that can grip, so jaws and hooves do none of the three.

**Unarmed Combat** — A limb held fast performs nothing, though it keeps hold of whatever it was carrying.

**Unarmed Combat** — The Folk Roster states a Grab's zone die and a Limb Block's reach.
