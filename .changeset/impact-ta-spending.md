**Rules and combat**

- Attacking players can choose Impact Tactical Advantages without rerolling damage, and explicit zero values now mean no bonus.
- The GM's Automated Combat tour demonstrates two exchanges and recording an S2 injury, and leaving or restarting a seeded tour restores ordinary rolls.
- The Automated Attack macro now starts the supported attack workflow.
- Crafting macros can offer player-chosen Value Diamond allocation after a roll, and explicitly selected workshops use their recorded star rating as the qualifier Target Number.

**Characters and sheets**

- Structure header fields now follow the sheet's editing permissions.
