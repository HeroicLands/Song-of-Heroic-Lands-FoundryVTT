**Metalcraft** — A smith's everyday work turns a profit again: shoeing a horse, driving a piton, and forging chain or a tin plate no longer cost more in iron than the finished piece sells for.

**Armour** — Every garment and suit's weight now sits in line with how much of the body it covers and what it is made of, instead of drifting from one article to the next within the same cloth or hide.
