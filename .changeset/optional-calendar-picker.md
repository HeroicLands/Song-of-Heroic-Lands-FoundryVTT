**Rules and combat**

- Entangling weapons offer a Hold alternative.
- A landed enveloping attack offers an owner-confirmed Enveloped condition, which penalizes attacks and defenses by 20 until removed.
- Pending automatic group assignment stops safely if its combat or combatant disappears, and reports failures while the combat remains live.

**Characters and sheets**

- Date fields can use an active optional calendar picker while retaining stored seconds.
- The date action offers the basic editor, current time, clearing, and cancellation; the basic editor remains available for leap days and editing seconds.
- Affiliation items keep organisational parents and seat, while each place states its governing body.
