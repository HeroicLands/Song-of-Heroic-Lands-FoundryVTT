A great coopered tun, chest-high and too heavy to move full by any means but a ramp and a rope. Brewers ferment in them, fishers salt down catch in them, and merchants ship everything from nails to salt pork in them. Once broached, a tun of this size must be used within days or spoil; the barrel outlives its contents many times over and is usually resold rather than broken up. 50 gallon capacity.
