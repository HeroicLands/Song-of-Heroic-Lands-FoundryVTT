Photophobia is a condition characterized by an intense sensitivity and light. People with photophobia may experience a range of symptoms when they are exposed to bright light.

### Behaviors

Wearing sunglasses indoors, keeping lights dim, or avoiding outdoor activities during daylight.

### Physical Symptoms

Squinting, headaches, nausea, dizziness, and pain in the eyes.
