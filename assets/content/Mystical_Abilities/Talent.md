Talent represents an innate psychic sensitivity—a natural aptitude for perceiving or influencing the world through channels that transcend ordinary sensory experience. Unlike the learned esoteric disciplines, Talent manifests as an inherent gift that may emerge unbidden and require practice to control rather than formal instruction to acquire. Practitioners may experience premonitions, empathic flashes, or subtle telekinetic effects. The strength of one's spiritual aura and force of will determine the potency of these abilities.
