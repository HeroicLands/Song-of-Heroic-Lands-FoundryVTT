**Spirit trauma** is harm to the part of a character that reaches past the body.
Neither of the two forms sits at a body location, so neither is rated as a wound
or resolved by an injury healing test.

[[doc-arlshck|Aural Shock]] is a temporary severance of the aura from what
anchors it — the backlash of a working gone wrong, the mark left by a hostile
spirit. Each instance is rated 1 to 6 and the levels from every source stack into
one running total, and while any of it remains every
[[doc-mysteryintro|Mystery]] the character holds is unavailable.

[[doc-thepall|The Pall]] is the gravest trauma in the rules. It emanates from the
forces of death and chaos, it is rated as a Pall Strength that falls off with
distance and daylight, and where the other trauma tests probe Will or Initiative
it contests **Spirit** for the character's soul. Losing that contest ends in
corruption into the undead rather than in recovery.

The two reach the other sections at their failures. A critical failure on an
Aural Shock recovery test generates Psyche Stress, which is resolved as a
[[doc-psychlgc|psychological condition]] under [[doc-mindtraumaintro|Mind]].
