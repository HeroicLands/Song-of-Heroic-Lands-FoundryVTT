**Mind trauma** is harm to a character's nerve and composure. The three pages
here divide into two tests and the lasting state they can leave behind.

[[doc-fear|Fear]] covers fear severe enough to demand a **Fear Test** against
Will — the threat of death or injury, arcane forces, the plainly unnatural.
[[doc-morale|Morale]] covers a fighter's willingness to stay in the fight, and
its **Morale Test** is a test of Initiative; most combatants lose heart before
their bodies give out, which is why nerve is tracked separately from wounds. Both
tests turn on which critical failure came up, and the `CF0` against `CF5`
distinction is defined on the Fear page for all of them.

A [[doc-psychlgc|psychological condition]] is the residue: a quirk, an impulse or
a disorder, rated in Psyche Stress Levels as a wound is rated in Injury Levels.
It rarely arises by itself. A critical failure on a Fear test or on an
[[doc-arlshck|Aural Shock]] recovery test is the usual source, so this page is
read after the two that generate it.

Fear hands off to the body as well: [[doc-shock|shock]] is brought on by fear and
other systemic forces exactly as it is by a wound, and it is resolved under
[[doc-bodytrmaintro|Body]].
