An **injury** is damage at one body location — a cut, a crushed bone, a burn —
and these pages carry it from the moment it is taken to the moment it closes or
kills. Read them in the order the wound itself moves through.

[[doc-injrylvl|Injury Levels]] rates the severity on a scale of 1 to 5.
[[doc-trtnginj|Treating Injuries]] is what a fresh wound needs before it can mend
at all: an untreated wound has no Healing Rate, and establishing one is what
treatment means. [[doc-hlngbs|Healing Base]] is the creature's own capacity to
recover and [[doc-hlngrt|Healing Rate]] is this wound's prospect, and the
[[doc-hlngtst|Healing Test]] rolls their product once per the wound's healing
period.

Three pages cover what a wound does besides heal. [[doc-imprmnt|Impairment]] is
the penalty it lays on every action using the body part it sits in.
[[doc-bleeding|Bleeding]] is the wound that kills in minutes unless it is
staunched. [[doc-infctn|Infection]] is what a critically failed Healing Test
starts, and it is recorded as harm of its own with a Healing Rate a step worse
than the wound that bred it.

[[doc-afflctnrules|Afflictions]] sits beside this section under the body, and the
pair are the clearest illustration of the distinction the
[[doc-traumaintro|Trauma]] introduction draws. An injury is a **state**: it is
done, it is rated, and it mends at a rate fixed when it was treated. An
affliction is a **process**: a disease or a poison is still acting, so its own
Course Test drives its Healing Rate up and down as it runs. An injury that
becomes infected does not turn into an affliction — the infection is recorded as
its own harm, and nothing is attacking from outside.
