**Body trauma** is harm to the flesh: what a blow, a poison, a forced march or a
cold night does to a character who has to go on using their body afterwards. The
section covers six kinds of it, and they are told apart by what they do rather
than by how they were come by.

An [[doc-injintro|injury]] is damage at a single body location, rated by severity
and resolved by healing or by death. An [[doc-afflctnrules|affliction]] is not a
state at all but a hostile agent — a disease, a poison, a maladiction — running
its own course toward cure or death. [[doc-fatigue|Fatigue]] is depletion rather
than damage, measured in Fatigue Levels and cleared by rest instead of
treatment. A [[doc-physclcn|physical condition]] is a standing fact of a
particular body — a limp, poor eyesight, a fortnight without food — which no
healing test resolves because nothing is mending.

[[doc-shock|Shock]] and [[doc-coma|coma]] are the two states a sudden,
overwhelming strain drives a body into, and they are read in that order: a
creature in shock makes a Shock Re-Test to shake it off, and only a critically
failed Re-Test sinks an unconscious victim into a coma.

Harm to the mind and to the spirit are their own sections —
[[doc-mindtraumaintro|Mind]] and [[doc-sprttraumaintro|Spirit]] — but they meet
the body here, because fear and other systemic forces bring on shock exactly as
a wound does.
