## Combat Techniques

These are the **Folk** unarmed techniques: the roster a folk character carries.
Other creatures do not use them — an animal, mythic, spirit, or construct carries
bespoke strike modes derived from its own anatomy, and most of what it carries
(claw, gore, beak, tail, quill) has no folk equivalent at all.

Where an entry is marked _Folk_ below, its impact dice describe a folk body part:
a folk bite is `1d4` piercing where a cave bear's is `1d6+9`. The unmarked ones
carry no impact dice — they are manoeuvres rather than body parts, and read
Strength and reach from whoever performs them, so they hold for any creature able
to perform them at all.

- [[Skills/bflkbite|Folk Bite]] - Teeth, at the range where nothing else will reach; small, precise, and it draws blood.
- [[Skills/bflkgrab|Grab]] - Seize a limb — to take the weapon out of it, or to hold it still.
- [[Skills/bflkheadbutt|Folk Headbutt]] - The forehead driven into a face, from too close for anything else to work.
- [[Skills/bflkkick|Folk Kick]] - A boot driven out — the longest reach a person has without a weapon.
- [[Skills/limbblock|Limb Block]] - Warding a blow with a limb — desperate bare-armed, deliberate in a mail sleeve.
- [[Skills/press|Press]] - A shove: putting an opponent where you want them, and sometimes on the ground.
- [[Skills/bflkpunch|Folk Punch]] - A closed fist — the plainest thing a person can do in a fight, and the weakest.
- [[Skills/trip|Trip]] - Taking the legs out from under an opponent, and the fight with them.
