A **skill** represents a character's trained ability at some activity — a
weapon, a craft, a lore, a social art. Each skill is rated by a
**Mastery Level (ML)**, and whenever the outcome of using a skill is uncertain,
the character makes a test against it.

The system emphasizes graduated outcomes over binary pass/fail. A character who
succeeds critically produces meaningfully better results than one who barely
succeeds, and a critical failure carries consequences beyond simple
non-achievement. This granularity extends to opposed situations, where the
margin of victory (measured in Victory Stars) often determines the severity of
the outcome.
