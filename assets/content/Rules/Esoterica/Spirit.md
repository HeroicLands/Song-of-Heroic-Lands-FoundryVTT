The **Spirit** tradition draws its power from spirits — ancestors, totems, and the wild energies of the world. A spirit-worker does not command power directly, as an arcanist does, nor petition a distant god, as a priest does; they cultivate relationships with spirits, who confer **Spirit Powers** on them. Those powers are the standing conduit through which every spirit rite and action is performed. This page collects the spirit **Mysteries** a character carries and the spirit **Mystical Abilities** they perform. For the shared framework — the Mystery-versus-Ability distinction, how abilities are tested, and the general mysteries (Boon, Boost, Other) — see the [[rules/sohl-esoterica|Esoterica Introduction]].

# Mysteries

The spirit tradition has no mystery peculiar to it in the way the [[rules/sohl-arcane|Arcane]] tradition has the Birthsign or the [[rules/sohl-divine|Divine]] has Piety — a spirit-worker's supernatural standing is expressed instead through the **Spirit Powers** the spirits have granted (described under Mystical Abilities below), which stand ready like a mystery even as they are exercised like an ability.

The tradition-agnostic mysteries — **Boon**, **Boost**, and **Other**, described in the [[rules/sohl-esoterica|Esoterica Introduction]] — may of course attach to a spirit skill, and a spirit patron is a common source of one.

# Mystical Abilities

Spirit acts a character performs. A **Spirit Power** is the standing gift a spirit confers; the **Spirit Rite** and **Spirit Action** are performed _through_ such a power, and their test is governed by it rather than by an ordinary skill — so neither can be used unless a valid Spirit Power is associated with it.

## Spirit Power

A standing power conferred on a character by a spirit — the enduring gift that Spirit Rites and Spirit Actions are performed through. Spirit Powers are the channels a spirit-worker has cultivated; each is granted by a particular kind of spirit, such as an **Ancestor**, a **Totem**, or one of the elemental **Energies**. A Spirit Power is less an action in itself than the readied conduit that makes spirit actions and rites possible.

## Spirit Rite

A prepared ceremony by which a practitioner petitions the spirit world. A rite might carry the practitioner's awareness across into the spirit world, beseech a spirit for some boon or intervention, or share the wisdom a spirit has imparted. A Spirit Rite is always performed _through_ a Spirit Power the practitioner holds, and its test is governed by that power — without a Spirit Power to work through, there is no rite to perform.

## Spirit Action

A discrete supernatural act carried out through an allied or bound spirit — the spirit-world counterparts of everyday deeds. Typical Spirit Actions include roaming the spirit world, sensing across it, and communing with the spirits within it. Like a Spirit Rite, a Spirit Action is exercised through an associated Spirit Power, which governs its test.

## Spirit Talent

An innate affinity for the spirit world, possessed without training — the spirit-world counterpart of an Arcane Talent. A character with a Spirit Talent can produce a spirit effect naturally, without having cultivated it through a spirit's tutelage.
