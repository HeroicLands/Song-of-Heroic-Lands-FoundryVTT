The **Divine** tradition draws its power from a deity, granted in answer to devotion. Divine practitioners do not command power so much as request it: the god acts, and the character's standing with that god governs how readily and how strongly the request is answered. This page collects the divine **Mysteries** a character carries and the divine **Mystical Abilities** they perform. For the shared framework — the Mystery-versus-Ability distinction, how abilities are tested, and the general mysteries (Boon, Boost, Other) — see the [[rules/sohl-esoterica|Esoterica Introduction]].

# Mysteries

The standing divine conditions that describe a character's relationship with their deity. Both measure something the character _is_, and together they colour how the divine answers. (The tradition-agnostic mysteries — Boon, Boost, and Other — are described in the [[rules/sohl-esoterica|Esoterica Introduction]] and may attach to a divine skill as well.)

## Grace

The measure of a character's ability to call effectually on divine favour — how readily, and how strongly, their god answers. Grace stands behind the divine abilities, colouring how a character's appeals to the divine are received.

## Piety

The measure of a character's devotion to their religion, and the standing that devotion earns them with their deity. Piety is the currency of the faithful: it rises and falls with observance and transgression, and it governs how freely divine power is granted in answer to prayer.

# Mystical Abilities

Divine acts a character performs. They range from a prescribed observance or a blessing asked in a moment to a great miracle called down through ritual; the **Divine Incantation** is subject to the **Incantation Casting Penalty** described in the [[rules/sohl-esoterica|Esoterica Introduction]] (a penalty to EML of **Level × 2**).

## Ritual Action

A prescribed ritual act performed to earn — or maintain — the favour of a deity. This covers the whole range of divine observances a character offers, from the everyday rites that keep them in good standing to a **blessing** (or, turned about, a curse) called down on a person or thing in answer to prayer. The correct rite, correctly performed, is what keeps a character in good standing with their god.

## Divine Incantation

A spoken invocation that channels the power of a deity, performed as a ritual that asks the divine to act. Divine Incantations are the great miracles of the faithful; the more powerful the miracle requested, the harder it is to call down (the Level × 2 Incantation Casting Penalty).
