A standing bonus (or penalty) to an associated skill — a flat adjustment to that skill's mastery level, from whatever source granted it. A Boon simply makes a character reliably better (or worse) at the skill it names, for as long as the character holds it. Because a Boon just shifts a skill up or down, it can attach to a skill of any tradition.
